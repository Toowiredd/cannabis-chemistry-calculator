### Added - 2026-07-25 (post-cross-tab batch)

- **IndexedDB mirror extended to journal entries** (commit `6f5653e`) - The PWA's IndexedDB shim (commit `97d90e6`) previously mirrored only presets and strains to the durable IDB layer. Journal entries were still localStorage-only — a Safari "Clear website data" sweep or a localStorage quota eviction would wipe the user's most important data (their saved batches). The new mirror stores a single `{ index, entries }` blob under `IDB_KEY.journalEntries`. `saveJournalEntry` and `deleteJournalEntry` await the IDB write (not fire-and-forget) to keep localStorage and IDB in lock-step across the read-modify-write cycle. `loadJournalEntries` falls back to the IDB blob when localStorage is empty (same pattern as `loadStrains`) and re-hydrates localStorage on the recovery read. The merge logic preserves IDB entries that aren't in localStorage as a defense against partial localStorage eviction. IDB write failure is non-fatal — the localStorage copy is the source of truth and the next save retries. 5 new tests in `web-shim.test.ts`: round-trip, IDB mirror, Safari eviction recovery, delete mirror, empty-state regression guard.
